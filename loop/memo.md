# Findings memo

This looks like a scam.
Police and banks never ask you to move money to a safe account.
It says it is from the police, court, tax office or similar.
1. Stop. Do not click, reply, pay or give any number.
2. Check. Call your bank using the number on the back of your card, or ask your family.
3. Report and block. Tell the official scam hotline, then block the number.
4. Official hotline: National Scam Response Centre (NSRC), 997.
